# Walkthrough: THE YELLOW CHASE

What a visitor experiences today on https://dontshakeme.vercel.app, from the first screen to the
game-over screen. Unofficial concept by @VOtakpor for @Pepsi_naija.

---

## Part 1: The intro

### 1. Same taste, new look
- Black screen. The Pepsi bottle with the **new yellow cap** (clean, ridged Pepsi-yellow plastic, the original cap's shape) spins in as the light comes up, with the blue halftone ring behind it.
- On screen: **SAME TASTE.** (white) / **NEW LOOK.** (yellow) above the bottle.
- The bottle looks **straight out of the fridge**: the plastic and label are fogged with condensation, with clear beads all over (bigger towards the bottom) that catch the light and bend the label behind them, clear trails where drops have run down, and beads slowly sliding down. On HD the label also has a wet gloss.
- There's **real cola inside**: near-black with a red-brown glow at the edges, a thin foam line at the top and the odd bubble rising.
- It's a **live 3D model**: drag anywhere to spin it (flick it and it coasts), drag up or down to tip it a little. Left alone, it turns slowly on its own. Arrow keys spin it on a computer.
- Tip it and the surface stays level. On **Android**, tilting or shaking the phone itself moves the cola too (iPhone: drag only, since we never show a motion-permission prompt).

### 2. Shake it, or PLAY
Beneath the bottle: **PLAY**, a **Leaderboard** link, and "best with sound" (and your best run, once you have one). Two ways in:
- **Shake it** (quick back-and-forth drags, or shake the phone on Android), as in real life:
  1. The first couple of seconds the cola **only sloshes**: no fizz, no sound.
  2. Keep shaking and it starts to **fizz**: bubbles stream up, you hear it, and a **head of foam climbs** up the shoulder towards the cap. Halfway there the title turns into **DON'T SHAKE ME.** (with a hiss) and the ring throbs. As with real shaken soda, the cola clouds with tiny bubbles and the foam fills the bottle **wall to wall** and settles **level** (it barely sloshes): coarse, darker bubbles where it meets the cola, fine creamy foam on top.
  3. Stop and it slowly settles (the foam sinks back). Keep shaking with the foam at the cap and it **blows**: POP, cap flash, blue shockwave, and the bottle lifts off out of the top of the frame, **shedding cola** that falls away. About 6 seconds of steady shaking end to end.
- **Tap PLAY** (or Enter / Space): the same thing at once: the foam surges up to the cap, **POP**, and the bottle lifts off, shedding cola. (No cinematic around the bottle any more: just the bottle and PLAY.)

### 3. Who catches it?
- Either way, once the bottle is gone: **WHO CATCHES IT?**
- First, **your name on the leaderboard** (2–16 letters or numbers). The first time it's empty and ready to type in; after that it's remembered and filled in (you can change it). Tapping a runner without a name doesn't start: "Type your name first".
- Then the two runners side by side. Tap one to open the street, where the bottle lands (Part 2).

## Part 2: The street scene (about 7 seconds; **SKIP** top right, or Esc)

**The story:** the bottle you set off lands in a Lagos street right by your runner. The agberos hanging about see the shining Golden Cap and want it. Your runner makes as if to hand it over... and bolts.

| Time | Beat | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 s | **It lands** | A Lagos street at sunset; the drums come back. Your runner is standing in the road, the agberos close by. The bottle flies in from where it left the intro, tumbling, and **lands upright in the road** just in front of your runner with a bounce and a wobble. |
| 1–2 s | **The shine** | Low and close on the bottle: the **cap flashes gold** with a ring of light and a shimmer. The boys jump at it. |
| 2–3 s | **The grab** | Your runner **crouches and picks it up**. |
| 3.5–6 s | **"Gimme am"** | Over your runner's shoulder, the lead agbero: **AGBERO:** "Afa, wetin be that wey dey shine? Gimme am ASAP!" |
| 6–7.4 s | **The offer** | Your runner turns to him, steps closer and **holds the bottle out**, as if to hand it over... |
| 7.4 s | **The bolt** | ...then whips round and **bursts away**. **AGBEROS:** "If I catch you!" A whistle, a trumpet fanfare and drum roll throw into the **theme song**, the camera swings in behind you, the crew gives chase and the controls come on. |

- **SKIP** at any point drops you straight into the run: bottle in hand, theme playing, camera behind you, controls live. (Pressed before the pick, the pick still comes first, then straight into the run.)
- **RUN AGAIN** never replays the cinematic.

## Part 3: The Yellow Chase (the game)

### 4. The run
An endless run down a three-lane Lagos street lined with shops, balconies and electric poles.

**Controls**

| Phone | Computer | Move |
|---|---|---|
| Swipe left / right | ← → or A / D | Change lane (with a swoosh) |
| Swipe up | ↑, W or Space | Jump (a swipe just before you land jumps as soon as you touch down) |
| Swipe down | ↓ or S | Slide (body thrown back, feet first). Mid-air, this slams you down into a slide. |
| Double-tap | B | Release the Pulse (when the meter is full) |

**Obstacles**

| Obstacle | How to beat it |
|---|---|
| Danfo bus, keke | Change lane |
| Okada | Change lane. **They ride towards you**, and you hear a *pim-pim* horn from their lane first. |
| Concrete barrier with cones, sachet-water table | Jump |
| Banner on poles | Slide under |

Every row of traffic always leaves you a way through. **Rows reach you on the beat of the theme song**: one every 3 beats at first (about 1.5 s), every 2 beats from 2,000 m.

**Pepsi branding:** billboards on the pavements ("STAY ON THE PULSE" / "SAME TASTE. NEW LOOK." with the Pepsi globe), well spaced: one every 60 m or so, switching sides (about every 3 seconds at full speed). The overhead banners you slide under carry a Pepsi print.

**Collectibles:** lines of **yellow Pepsi caps**, some arcing over the jumps. They're spaced one per 16th note of the song, so a streak of chimes runs in time with it, climbing in pitch.

**Speed:** 17 m/s, stepping up at **500 m, 1,200 m, 2,000 m and 3,000 m** ("SPEED UP!") to 24 m/s. The song keeps its own tempo.

**The chase builds with the distance** (no dialogue: the music and the cap carry it):

| Distance | Golden Cap | Music layered on the theme |
|---|---|---|
| 0 m | | The theme on its own |
| 500 m | | Agogo bell, brass accents every 4 bars |
| 1,200 m | Starts glowing blue **on every beat** | + talking-drum fills answering every 2 bars |
| 2,000 m | Stronger glow | + brass every 2 bars with pickups, a riser into every 8th bar |
| 3,000 m | Strongest glow | Full: brass every bar, fills every bar, shaker 16ths |

**On screen during the run:** distance in metres and speed stage (top left), caps and the **PULSE** meter (top right), and a **pause** button in the top bar.

**Pause:** the pause button (or P / Esc on a computer) freezes the run and the music exactly where they are; leaving the page (switching apps, locking the phone) pauses too. The pause screen has **RESUME** (a big **3, 2, 1** on screen, then the run and the song carry on exactly in step), **RESTART RUN** (a fresh run; the paused one isn't recorded) and the **Leaderboard**.

### 5. The agberos
- They run right behind you, then drop back out of view until you make a mistake.
- **Once the run starts they say nothing except:** "You dey use me play, abi?!" every time you **stumble**, and "Ha! Wetin be that?!" the **first time you release the Pulse** in a run. The line sits over the speaker's head, or at the bottom of the screen with their name when they're out of view ("CHAIRMAN: …").
- **Near misses** (a late swerve past traffic, or a close jump / slide) get a whoosh past your ear.

### 6. Stumbles and getting caught
- **Clip the side of something** and you **stumble**. The **Chairman surges right up behind you**, reaching for the Pepsi: **"You dey use me play, abi?!"**
- **A second stumble within 4 seconds**, or **running head-on into anything**, and you're caught.

### 7. Pulse Mode (the Golden Cap's power)
- **Charging: it's worked for over many runs.** Every cap you grab, in every run, is **saved** (in your browser) towards the Pulse, and the **PULSE** meter shows your saved caps against the target ("PULSE 1,240/2,000"). The **first Pulse needs 2,000 saved caps**; releasing it spends them, and the **next needs 4,000**, then 6,000, 8,000 and so on. Caps keep saving while it's ready or running, so they count towards the next one. A good run brings in roughly 3–4 caps a second. The meter throbs faster as it fills. When full it turns electric blue and reads **PULSE READY** ("double-tap" underneath), a rising fizz-and-bass sound plays, a blue ring breathes on the road under your runner, and the bottle in their hand glows.
- **Release (double-tap, or B on a computer):** a split-second **wind-up** (the view draws in, the bottle flares, an intake swell), then **RELEASE THE PULSE.**
  - Concentric electric-blue shockwave rings with a white-hot core race out across the road, with a burst of carbonation bubbles, a camera jolt, a quick blue flash and a deep bass drop with a fizzy release.
  - The agberos are blown back and stagger. The first time in a run, CHAIRMAN: "Ha! Wetin be that?!"
  - Traffic and barriers just ahead are flung aside (each one only once).
  - Caps ahead fly straight to you.
- **Then you keep running for about 8 seconds** (16 beats, four bars of the song). No floating, no foam: a ring pulses out from your feet **on every beat**, flinging away anything within about 26 m ahead, caps keep flying to you and the street light pulses with it. A sub-bass thump joins the song on each beat. **STAY ON THE PULSE** shows under the score and a **timer bar** under the meter runs down. Nothing can hit you, and you can still change lanes, jump and slide.
- After the last ring you get half a second of protection and the meter starts again from empty. Double-tapping with an empty meter does nothing.
- On **LITE**, the gameplay is identical, with fewer rings and bubbles, a softer flash and no camera-facing burst.

### 8. Caught: game over
- A crash, and the crowd yells: **"We don catch am!"** The theme drops low under them and a deflating trumpet plays ("wah, wah, wah, waaah").
- The camera swings round to the front for the **trophy shot**: your runner sits defeated on the road while the Chairman **holds up your Pepsi** and the boys gather round. A big **Pepsi bottle with the yellow cap** spins in the corner.
- Then the Golden Cap answers back: it **rattles and sparks** blue in his hand, and the Chairman isn't so sure: **"Ehn? Wetin dey inside this cap sef?"**
- The game-over panel shows:
  - **AGBEROS DON COLLECT AM!**
  - Your **metres**, **caps** and **best** distance
  - Your place on the **leaderboard**: "Leaderboard: #61 of 340 (best 1,234 m)" (your best run counts)
  - Your caps **saved for the Pulse** ("Saved for the Pulse: 1,240 / 2,000 caps"), or **PULSE READY for your next run**
  - **RUN AGAIN**: straight back into the chase (whistle, swing, go, no cinematic); the song comes back up
  - **CHALLENGE FRIENDS**: opens X with a ready-made post:
    > I dodged the agberos for 1,234m and grabbed 87 yellow caps with the new yellow-cap Pepsi @Pepsi_naija! Can you beat my score? #PepsiYellowChase
    >
    > https://dontshakeme.vercel.app
  - **LEADERBOARD** (see below)
  - **STAY ON THE PULSE** with the Pepsi logo
  - **Watch the intro again**: back to SAME TASTE. NEW LOOK.


### 9. The leaderboard
- Opens from the intro (**Leaderboard** under PLAY), the pause screen and the game-over panel.
- **The top 50** longest runs: rank, name, distance (gold, silver and bronze ranks for the top three), with how many runners are on the board.
- If you're further down than 50: a **"…"** after the 50th, then **your own row** with your rank (e.g. "61 · Ada Okafor (you) · 52 m"). If you're in the top 50, your row is highlighted where it is.
- Every run you finish is sent; only your **best** distance counts. Names that are rude, or that pose as Pepsi, aren't accepted (you're asked for another name before your next run).
- Runs are checked by the server: each run gets a signed token when it starts, used once, and the distance has to be possible in the time since (top speed 24 m/s), so a made-up score can't simply be posted.
- Until the leaderboard's storage is connected on Vercel, it says "The leaderboard isn't open yet" and everything else works as normal.

## Always on screen
- **LITE / HD** (top left): lighter graphics for slower phones (simpler lighting, slightly lower resolution, a shorter street in the fog, the crew holding back at the start; same gameplay). Weak phones switch to it automatically, also mid-run.
- **Speaker** (top right): mute/unmute (music, effects and voices), remembered for your next visit.
- **"Unofficial concept by @VOtakpor"** (bottom left).

## Placeholders, to be replaced
- **Agbero voices:** generated in code until the voice-over is recorded (`public/audio/agbero-1.mp3` … `agbero-6.mp3`).
- **Music:** the owner's theme song, "Morning Train to Somewhere" (`public/audio/theme.mp3`, 124 BPM, D minor). Swapping it means re-measuring the beat grid in `src/audio/music.ts`. The cinematic score and the chase intensity layers (talking drum, agogo, trumpet, brass) are generated in code (`src/audio/score.ts`).
- **Voices:** every line of dialogue is a speech bubble; the agbero voices under them are generated placeholders, and the runner's line has no voice yet.
- **Running on the danfo roofs via ramps:** planned, not built yet.
