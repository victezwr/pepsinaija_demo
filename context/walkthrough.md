# Walkthrough: THE YELLOW CHASE

What a visitor experiences today on https://dontshakeme.vercel.app, from the first screen to the
game-over screen. Unofficial concept by @VOtakpor for @Pepsi_naija.

---

## Part 1: The intro

### 1. Same taste, new look
- Black screen. The Pepsi bottle with the **new yellow cap** spins in as the light comes up, with the blue halftone ring behind it.
- On screen: **SAME TASTE.** (white) / **NEW LOOK.** (yellow) above the bottle.
- The bottle looks **straight out of the fridge**: misted plastic, a fogged label with clear trails where drops have run down, water beads all round, and (on HD) a wet gloss and beads slowly sliding down.
- It's a **live 3D model**: drag anywhere to spin it (flick it and it coasts), drag up or down to tip it a little. Left alone, it turns slowly on its own. Arrow keys spin it on a computer.

### 2. Pick your runner, PLAY
- Beneath the bottle: your **runner** (male / female thumbnails) either side of **PLAY**, with "best with sound" (and your best run, once you have one).
- **Tap PLAY** (or Enter / Space) to start the opening cinematic.

## Part 2: The opening cinematic (about 20 seconds; **SKIP** top right, or Esc)

**The story:** the Golden Cap holds the energy behind PULSE MODE. You set it off by accident, the blue burst catches the eye of the Chairman and his two boys at the motor park, the Chairman decides the cap is his, and you refuse.

| Time | Beat | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **The awakening** | The lights go out. A low dùndún talking-drum groove starts. A blue rim light finds the bottle as the camera eases in. **SOME THINGS SHOULD STAY CLOSED.** Three short, suspicious trumpet notes. |
| 3–7 s | **The Golden Cap** | The camera circles the bottle. The yellow cap glows **gold** for a moment (with a shimmer), a restrained blue energy ripple pulses out. **DON'T SHAKE ME.** Then the music stops dead. |
| 7–9 s | **The pop** | The bottle trembles as pressure hisses up, then **POP**: the cap flashes, a big blue shockwave rings out (a taste of PULSE MODE), and the bottle rockets up out of the frame. |
| 9–16 s | **Chairman notices** | A Lagos motor park at sunset; the drums come back. The bottle drops into your runner's hand and the Golden Cap flares gold. The boys jump at the flash. **AGBERO:** "Chairman, you see that thing?" The camera pushes in: **CHAIRMAN:** "Ha! Golden Cap! Omo, bring that one come!" |
| 16–20 s | **The chase** | Your runner turns to face them: **"Na drink I come buy!"** **CHAIRMAN:** "Oya! No let am escape!" A whistle, a trumpet fanfare and drum roll throw into the **theme song**, the camera swings in behind you and the controls come on. |

- **SKIP** at any point drops you straight into the run: bottle in hand, theme playing, camera behind you, controls live.
- **RUN AGAIN** never replays the cinematic.

## Part 3: The Yellow Chase (the game)

### 3. The run
An endless run down a three-lane Lagos street lined with shops, balconies and electric poles.

**Controls**

| Phone | Computer | Move |
|---|---|---|
| Swipe left / right | ← → or A / D | Change lane (with a swoosh) |
| Swipe up | ↑, W or Space | Jump |
| Swipe down | ↓ or S | Slide (body thrown back, feet first). Mid-air, this slams you down into a slide. |
| Double-tap | B | Release the Pulse (when the meter is full) |

**Obstacles**

| Obstacle | How to beat it |
|---|---|
| Danfo bus, keke | Change lane |
| Okada | Change lane. **They ride towards you**, and you hear a *pim-pim* horn from their lane first. |
| Concrete barrier with cones, sachet-water table | Jump |
| Banner on poles | Slide under |

Every row of traffic always leaves you a way through. **Rows reach you on the beat of the theme song**: one every 4 beats at first, every 3 beats from 2,000 m.

**Collectibles:** lines of **yellow Pepsi caps**, some arcing over the jumps. They're spaced one per 16th note of the song, so a streak of chimes runs in time with it, climbing in pitch.

**Speed:** 17 m/s, stepping up at **500 m, 1,200 m, 2,000 m and 3,000 m** ("SPEED UP!") to 24 m/s. The song keeps its own tempo.

**The story keeps going with the distance** (and the music builds over the theme):

| Distance | Story | Music layered on the theme |
|---|---|---|
| 0 m | Chase starts: "Bring that cap back!" | The theme on its own |
| 500 m | The crew realise you're hard to catch: "Chairman, this one no dey tire o!" | Agogo bell, brass accents every 4 bars |
| 1,200 m | The Golden Cap in your hand starts glowing blue **on every beat** | + talking-drum fills answering every 2 bars |
| 2,000 m | Stronger cap glow. CHAIRMAN: "All of una, run! Make una catch am!" | + brass every 2 bars with pickups, a riser into every 8th bar |
| 3,000 m | Strongest cap glow. A nervous agbero: "Abeg, make we calm down small." | Full: brass every bar, fills every bar, shaker 16ths |

**On screen during the run:** distance in metres and speed stage (top left), caps and the **PULSE** meter (top right).

### 4. The agberos
- They run right behind you, shouting pidgin in speech bubbles over the speaker's head: "Hold am!", "Where you dey go?!", "Oya drop am!", "Bring that cap come!", "Stop there!"
- After a few seconds they drop back out of view, until you make a mistake. When a speaker is out of view, their line shows at the bottom of the screen with their name ("CHAIRMAN: …").
- **Near misses** (a late swerve past traffic, or a close jump / slide) get a whoosh past your ear. The first one each run: "Chairman, e dey escape!"; after that, now and then: "You think say you fit run pass me?"
- Lines are tied to what happens, at most one at a time, with one-time lines and cooldowns so they never pile up.

### 5. Stumbles and getting caught
- **Clip the side of something** and you **stumble**. The **Chairman surges right up behind you**, reaching for the Pepsi: **"Oya drop am!"**
- **A second stumble within 4 seconds**, or **running head-on into anything**, and you're caught.

### 6. Pulse Mode (the Golden Cap's power)
- **Charging:** every cap fills the **PULSE** meter. It throbs faster as it fills. At **50 caps** it turns electric blue and reads **PULSE READY** ("double-tap" underneath), a rising fizz-and-bass sound plays, a blue ring breathes on the road under your runner, and the bottle in their hand glows.
- **Release (double-tap, or B on a computer):** a split-second **wind-up** (the view draws in, the bottle flares, an intake swell), then **RELEASE THE PULSE.**
  - Concentric electric-blue shockwave rings with a white-hot core race out across the road, with a burst of carbonation bubbles, a camera jolt, a quick blue flash and a deep bass drop with a fizzy release.
  - The agberos are blown back and stagger. CHAIRMAN: "Ha! Wetin be that?!"
  - Traffic and barriers just ahead are flung aside (each one only once).
  - Caps ahead fly straight to you.
- **Then you keep running for about 8 seconds** (21 beats of the song). No floating, no foam: a ring pulses out from your feet **on every beat**, flinging away anything within about 26 m ahead, caps keep flying to you and the street light pulses with it. A sub-bass thump joins the song on each beat. **STAY ON THE PULSE** shows under the score and a **timer bar** under the meter runs down. Nothing can hit you, and you can still change lanes, jump and slide.
- After the last ring you get half a second of protection and the meter starts again from empty. The agberos, left far behind: "Chairman, this one get power o!" Double-tapping with an empty meter does nothing.
- On **LITE**, the gameplay is identical, with fewer rings and bubbles, a softer flash and no camera-facing burst.

### 7. Caught: game over
- A crash, and the crowd yells: **"We don catch am!"** The theme drops low under them and a deflating trumpet plays ("wah, wah, wah, waaah").
- The camera swings round to the front for the **trophy shot**: your runner sits defeated on the road while the Chairman **holds up your Pepsi** and the boys gather round. A big **Pepsi bottle with the yellow cap** spins in the corner.
- Then the Golden Cap answers back: it **rattles and sparks** blue in his hand, and the Chairman isn't so sure: **"Ehn? Wetin dey inside this cap sef?"**
- The game-over panel shows:
  - **AGBEROS DON COLLECT AM!**
  - Your **metres**, **caps** and **best** distance
  - **RUN AGAIN**: straight back into the chase (whistle, swing, go); the song comes back up
  - **CHALLENGE FRIENDS**: opens X with a ready-made post:
    > I dodged the agberos for 1,234m and grabbed 87 yellow caps with the new yellow-cap Pepsi @Pepsi_naija! Can you beat my score? #PepsiYellowChase
    >
    > https://dontshakeme.vercel.app
  - **STAY ON THE PULSE** with the Pepsi logo
  - **Watch the intro again**: back to SAME TASTE. NEW LOOK.

## Always on screen
- **LITE / HD** (top left): lighter graphics for slower phones. Weak phones switch to it automatically.
- **Speaker** (top right): mute/unmute (music, effects and voices), remembered for your next visit.
- **"Unofficial concept by @VOtakpor"** (bottom left).

## Placeholders, to be replaced
- **Agbero voices:** generated in code until the voice-over is recorded (`public/audio/agbero-1.mp3` … `agbero-6.mp3`).
- **Music:** the owner's theme song (`public/audio/theme.mp3`). Swapping it means re-measuring the beat grid in `src/audio/music.ts`. The cinematic score and the chase intensity layers (talking drum, agogo, trumpet, brass) are generated in code (`src/audio/score.ts`).
- **Voices:** every line of dialogue is a speech bubble; the agbero voices under them are generated placeholders, and the runner's line has no voice yet.
- **Agbero hand props:** pending a decision.
- **Running on the danfo roofs via ramps:** planned, not built yet.
